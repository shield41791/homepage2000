Sticky-feeling footer bar with a spinning vinyl-disc icon, scrolling track name, and play/mute toggles. No real playback — it's a period-accurate UI shell.

```jsx
<FooterPlayer trackLabel="쭈요's 로파이 플레이리스트 - track 01" playing muted={false}
  onTogglePlay={togglePlay} onToggleMute={toggleMute} onToggleSettings={toggleSettings}
```
